# Seasonal Forecasting of Ferry Passenger Demand

## Overview
Time series forecasting research on ferry passenger demand at Bakauheni Port, Indonesia, published in the Indonesian Journal of Electrical and Computer Science.

## Problem
Accurate passenger demand forecasting is essential for ferry scheduling, resource allocation, and operational efficiency at ports.

## Method
- Time series analysis
- Seasonal decomposition
- ARIMA/SARIMA modeling
- Feature engineering for temporal patterns
- Model evaluation with multiple metrics

## Result
Published research in the Indonesian Journal of Electrical and Computer Science. Model provides accurate seasonal forecasts for ferry passenger demand.

## Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas
- Scikit-learn
- Statsmodels
- Time series analysis tools
